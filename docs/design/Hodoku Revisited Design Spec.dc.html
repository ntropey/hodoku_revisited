01

Brand

The mark is a ruled 3×3 box with pencil marks in place. The candidates 1 3 4 5 6 7 9 sit in their fixed slots and spell an H. The 5 in the centre is accent blue — the one you're about to place. The grid lines are what make it read as sudoku, not dice.

CONSTRUCTIONA square box ruled into a 3×3 grid. The outer rule is about 3× the inner rules, the way box lines are drawn on the board. Digits are Plex Mono 600 at 20% of the mark's width. Corner radius is 6%.
CLEAR SPACEKeep one cell-width of space on every side. Don't place the mark inside another box.

Techniques / Intersections

Locked Candidates

Medium Type 1 · Pointing Type 2 · Claiming

A box and a row (or column) overlap in three cells. If a digit can only go in that overlap for one of them, it can't go anywhere else in the other.

Type 1 · Pointing

In box 3 the digit 4 only fits in r1c7 and r1c9, both in row 1. Wherever the 4 goes in box 3, it uses up row 1. So the 4 at r1c6 can be eliminated.

NOTATION Locked Candidates Type 1 (Pointing): 4 in b3 ⇒ r1c6<>4

Type 2 · Claiming

This is the same overlap read the other way round. If a row's candidates for a digit all sit in one box, you can eliminate that digit from the rest of the box…

Hints

A hint is the next step the solver would take from your current position. It names the technique, highlights the cells involved, strikes out what it removes, and links to the guide. “Hints use” limits which techniques it may use.

Breakpoints. Under 960px the side panel turns into a bottom sheet with three snap points: peek, half and full. Under 600px the top bar collapses to a menu, the difficulty badge and undo. Board. ACCESSIBILITY · MOTION Keep role="grid", the arrow keys and the aria-live status, which are already in place. Extend each cell's aria-label to read its candidates too (“row 1 column 6, candidates 3 4 5”). Animate only opacity and colour, for 120ms. Applying a step fades eliminated candidates over 200ms.
